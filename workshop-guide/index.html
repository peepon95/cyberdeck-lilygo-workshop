WHAT TO BRING

Your Cyber Deck toolkit.

01

LILYGO T-Display-S3

Standard non-touch 1.9-inch model with the 170×320 ST7789 display, used in 320×170 landscape mode.

02

USB-C data cable

Use a cable that supports data and charging. Match the laptop end: USB-C or USB-A.

03

Laptop

A Mac or Windows laptop that allows you to install Codex or Claude Code and PlatformIO.

04

Phone + hotspot

Internet for the AI tool and initial downloads. Keep your phone charged.

05

USB power bank

A normal 5V USB power bank lets you run the finished Cyber Deck away from your laptop.

Important: a charging-only USB-C cable can power the screen but cannot flash firmware. Bring a known data cable.
